About / boilerplate

Specularis is an AI visibility agency (GEO/AEO) founded in 2019 and based in Miami, Florida. It helps businesses get found, trusted, and cited by AI engines like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity through technical optimization, entity and authority building, and earned media. Specularis publishes a monthly scoreboard of its own AI visibility and offers free tools, including an AI Visibility Audit and an AI Citation Finder, at specularisinc.com.

Story angles

  • AI doesn't recommend businesses. It recommends sources.When you ask AI for "the best" in a category, it repeats the pages it trusts. If you're not in those pages, a competitor gets named instead.
  • An AI-visibility agency's own name got mistaken for a Roman rock.Asked "what is Specularis," AI described a translucent mineral the Romans used as window glass — because the mineral had a clear online identity and the company didn't. They're fixing it in public.
  • Most local businesses have their front door locked to AI.In a calibration set, two-thirds of the businesses tested were blocking AI crawlers at the network level without knowing it.
  • The transparency scoreboard.An agency running its own AI-visibility fix in the open, posting the monthly climb from zero — rare in a field full of black-box claims.
